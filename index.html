About Wavy

What is Wavy?

Wavy is an ocean hazard detection and alert platform built to protect coastal communities across India. We combine real-time weather data, satellite imagery from ISRO, IMD forecasts and community reports to give the earliest possible warnings of ocean-related disasters.

Whether you're a fisherman, a coastal resident, a disaster relief authority or a government body — Wavy gives you the information you need to act fast and stay safe.

✅ Real-time hazard tracking on interactive maps
✅ Community-powered hazard reporting
✅ Direct links to govt aid, donations & recovery
✅ Weather forecasting for 5 major coastal cities

Geographic Hazard Map

Interactive real-time map showing all active hazards with severity levels, spread radius and live updates.

Cyclone Tracking

Track cyclone path, wind speed, landfall ETA and affected zones — updated every 6 hours from IMD data.

Community Reporting
